A cash flow statement tracks the movement of cash into and out of a business during a specific period.

It helps businesses understand how well they manage their cash.

Cash flow is divided into three categories:

Operating Activities:
Cash generated from normal business operations.

Investing Activities:
Cash used for buying or selling assets such as equipment.

Financing Activities:
Cash received from loans, investors, or paid as dividends.

Positive cash flow indicates that a company has enough liquidity to run its operations.
