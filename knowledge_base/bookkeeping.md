Bookkeeping is the process of recording financial transactions of a business in a systematic manner.

These transactions include:
- Sales
- Purchases
- Payments received
- Payments made
- Expenses

Bookkeeping helps businesses maintain accurate financial records and track their financial activities.

Good bookkeeping ensures that businesses can monitor their income and expenses and prepare financial statements easily.

Bookkeeping is an essential part of accounting and helps maintain transparency in financial reporting.
