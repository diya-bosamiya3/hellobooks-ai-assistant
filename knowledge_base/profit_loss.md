A Profit and Loss (P&L) statement is a financial report that shows a company’s revenues, expenses, and profit or loss during a specific period.

The statement helps businesses evaluate their financial performance.

Key components include:
- Revenue (income earned)
- Cost of goods sold
- Operating expenses
- Net profit or loss

Profit is calculated using the formula:

Profit = Total Revenue - Total Expenses

If expenses exceed revenue, the business incurs a loss.

The P&L statement helps management make financial decisions and improve profitability.
