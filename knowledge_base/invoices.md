An invoice is a document issued by a seller to a buyer requesting payment for goods or services provided.

Invoices usually contain the following information:
- Invoice number
- Seller details
- Buyer details
- Description of goods or services
- Quantity and price
- Total amount payable
- Payment terms
- Due date

Invoices are important for bookkeeping because they record sales transactions and help track payments received from customers.

Businesses use invoices to maintain financial records and manage accounts receivable.
